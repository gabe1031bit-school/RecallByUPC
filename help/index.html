RecallByUPC

Hello! This is Recall by UPC, a web app that uses your camera to scan the product's barcode, and looks at many government agencies, such as the FDA, CSPC, the USDA, and many more government agencies to check if there are any product recalls for that specific item. Thank you! :)

Help Guide

Step One

Make sure you see your surrondings or yourself or anything in the box. If you don't, check the red text. If you are still stuck, check the helpful tip above the Flip! button.

Step Two

Get your item, and put its barcode in front of the camera. On mobile devices, you may "flip" the camera to your desired area, front or back, for a better scan, if the two cameras are of different quality. When you scan your desired item, if it is a valid UPC, then it gets passed onto our servers, where it will come back to you in under 300ms.

That's it! Thanks for using my product.
